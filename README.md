## 📈 Key Insights (Executive Summary)

Based on the analysis, several critical factors influencing customer churn were identified:

* **Contract Type:** Customers on month-to-month contracts are significantly more likely to churn compared to those on one- or two-year contracts.
* **Service Tenure:** The highest churn rate is observed within the first 6–12 months of the customer lifecycle.
* **Payment Method:** Electronic check users show a higher tendency to leave than those using automated bank transfers or credit cards.
* **Monthly Charges:** High monthly costs without bundled discounts are a major pain point for departing customers.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Introduce loyalty incentives for customers transitioning from the first 6 months of service.
* Encourage long-term contracts through targeted promotional offers.
* Improve the onboarding process for users of electronic payment methods to increase engagement.
